⚙️ Settings

Bazaar Sales is designed to run offline after it has been installed and opened once while online. Your product list and sales records are stored locally in this browser profile on this device.

Data and privacy

  • No account or cloud sync is required.
  • Clearing this browser's site data may erase saved products and sales.
  • Use Export Excel in Sales History to keep a copy of your records.

For a clean start, this mobile edition uses its own storage keys and begins with no products and no sales.
